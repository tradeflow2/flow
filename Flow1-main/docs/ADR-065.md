# ADR-065: Payment, Financial Confirmation and Customer Credit Architecture

**Project:** Flow1  
**Stage:** 6  
**Phase:** MVP+  
**Status:** Proposed for acceptance  
**Date:** 2026-11-15  
**Migration:** 008  
**Related ADRs:** ADR-012, ADR-013, ADR-014, ADR-026, ADR-028, ADR-036, ADR-045, ADR-064

## 1. Context

Flow1 requires a controlled financial workflow after Pre-Invoice issuance.

The system must support:
- Multiple payment methods for one Pre-Invoice.
- Partial payments.
- Independent financial confirmation.
- Minimal cheque lifecycle management.
- Customer credit agreements and outstanding credit exposure.
- Immutable financial history.

Flow1 is a general cross-trade B2B application. Payment and credit rules must not be hard-coded for a particular industry or company.

## 2. Confirmed Product Decisions

1. MVP payment methods are CASH, BANK_TRANSFER and CHEQUE.
2. Multiple payment methods may be combined for one Pre-Invoice.
3. Partial payments are supported.
4. Actual customer refunds are outside MVP.
5. Cheque lifecycle is limited to RECEIVED, DEPOSITED, CLEARED and BOUNCED.
6. Payment does not authorize physical release.
7. Flow1 does not replace formal accounting systems.

## 3. S6-A: Payments

### 3.1 Data Model: payments

Proposed fields:

- id
- organization_id
- pre_invoice_id
- payment_method
- amount
- payment_date
- reference_number
- payer_name
- bank_name
- cheque_number
- cheque_due_date
- cheque_status
- status
- notes
- created_by
- created_at
- confirmed_by
- confirmed_at

Money values follow ADR-058: whole Rial, NUMERIC(20,0), with no floating-point calculations.

Method-specific fields must be validated:
- CASH: no cheque fields.
- BANK_TRANSFER: bank and transaction reference where applicable.
- CHEQUE: cheque number, bank and due date.

### 3.2 Payment Rules

- Every payment belongs to exactly one organization and one Pre-Invoice.
- One Pre-Invoice may have multiple payment records.
- A payment cannot be assigned to multiple Pre-Invoices in MVP.
- Amount must be positive.
- Total allocated payment and credit amounts cannot exceed the Pre-Invoice total.
- Registered payments cannot be physically deleted.
- Confirmed payment data cannot be silently edited.
- Corrections require a traceable reversal or a new record.

### 3.3 Payment Status

- PENDING_CONFIRMATION
- CONFIRMED
- REJECTED
- REVERSED

Permitted transitions:

PENDING_CONFIRMATION → CONFIRMED  
PENDING_CONFIRMATION → REJECTED  
CONFIRMED → REVERSED

REJECTED and REVERSED are final states.

### 3.4 Cheque Lifecycle

Cheque status is independent from the general payment status:

- RECEIVED
- DEPOSITED
- CLEARED
- BOUNCED

A cheque is not considered collected money until CLEARED.

A BOUNCED cheque must remove its pending settlement effect and restore the corresponding outstanding amount. The event must be auditable.

### 3.5 Core

`core/tradeflow_core/payment.py`

Responsibilities:
- Payment validation.
- Method-specific validation.
- Amount and allocation validation.
- Payment state transitions.
- Cheque lifecycle validation.
- Reversal eligibility.
- Financial precision.

### 3.6 API

`payment_service.py`

Proposed routes:

- POST /api/v1/pre-invoices/{id}/payments
- GET /api/v1/pre-invoices/{id}/payments
- GET /api/v1/payments/{id}

## 4. S6-B: Financial Confirmation

### 4.1 Responsibilities

Financial confirmation is separate from payment registration.

The financial role must be able to:
- Review a pending payment.
- Confirm a valid payment.
- Reject an invalid payment with a reason.
- Review payment history.
- Record cheque lifecycle events.
- Reverse an eligible payment with an auditable reason.

### 4.2 Core

`core/tradeflow_core/financial_confirmation.py`

Responsibilities:
- Validate confirmation eligibility.
- Prevent duplicate confirmation.
- Enforce maker-checker separation.
- Validate rejection and reversal.
- Preserve transition history.
- Validate cheque events.

The person who registered a payment must not confirm that same payment.

### 4.3 API

`financial_service.py`

Proposed routes:

- POST /api/v1/payments/{id}/confirm
- POST /api/v1/payments/{id}/reject
- POST /api/v1/payments/{id}/reverse
- POST /api/v1/payments/{id}/cheque-events
- GET /api/v1/payments/{id}/history

### 4.4 History

Payment status and cheque lifecycle changes must be append-only.

Each event records:
- Payment ID
- Event type
- Previous state
- New state
- Actor
- Timestamp
- Reason, where required

The implementation may use a dedicated payment event/history table.

## 5. S6-C: Customer Credit

### 5.1 Data Model: credit_agreements

Proposed fields:

- id
- organization_id
- customer_id
- credit_limit
- credit_days
- valid_from
- valid_until
- guarantee_type
- guarantee_reference
- status
- created_by
- approved_by
- created_at
- approved_at

Proposed agreement states:

- DRAFT
- PENDING_APPROVAL
- ACTIVE
- SUSPENDED
- EXPIRED
- CLOSED

Only ACTIVE agreements may support new credit allocations.

Guarantee details are optional metadata in MVP. Guarantee issuance, custody and enforcement workflows are outside MVP.

### 5.2 Data Model: credit_transactions

Proposed fields:

- id
- organization_id
- credit_agreement_id
- pre_invoice_id
- payment_id
- transaction_type
- amount
- transaction_date
- due_date
- reverses_transaction_id
- created_by
- created_at
- reason

MVP transaction types:

- CREDIT_SALE
- PAYMENT_APPLIED
- REVERSAL

Transactions are immutable. Corrections use compensating entries.

The current outstanding balance is calculated from the transaction ledger. A manually editable balance is not the source of truth.

### 5.3 Credit Rules

- Credit sale requires an ACTIVE agreement.
- Credit allocation must not exceed the Pre-Invoice outstanding amount.
- Credit exposure must remain within the agreement's available limit.
- Credit days determine the due date.
- Payment confirmation or cheque clearance may reduce outstanding credit.
- A bounced cheque must not reduce the customer's outstanding debt.
- Credit allocation and limit validation must be atomic and concurrency-safe.
- Agreement changes must not rewrite historical transactions.

### 5.4 Core

`core/tradeflow_core/credit.py`

Responsibilities:
- Agreement validation.
- Credit eligibility.
- Available credit calculation.
- Credit allocation.
- Due-date calculation.
- Ledger posting.
- Payment application.
- Reversal handling.
- Concurrency-safe limit enforcement.

### 5.5 API

`credit_service.py`

## 6. Mixed Payment and Credit Allocation

A Pre-Invoice may be funded through multiple payment records and a credit allocation.

Example:

Pre-Invoice total: 100,000,000 Rial

- Cash: 20,000,000
- Bank transfer: 30,000,000
- Cleared cheque: 20,000,000
- Credit allocation: 30,000,000

Total allocation: 100,000,000 Rial.

Pending cheques are tracked separately from collected funds. A pending cheque is neither collected cash nor a completed credit payment.

The system must distinguish:
- Collected amount.
- Pending cheque amount.
- Credit obligation.
- Unallocated outstanding amount.

A bounced cheque returns its amount to the outstanding balance. It does not silently create a new credit agreement or credit allocation.

## 7. Existing Customer Credit Limit

The existing `customers.credit_limit` field must be reconciled with `credit_agreements`.

The new credit agreement model must be the authoritative source for credit limits.

Migration 008 must explicitly preserve and reconcile existing non-zero customer credit limits. It must not silently discard existing values or create two competing sources of truth.

## 8. Authorization

Permissions must be enforced by the backend.

Proposed permission capabilities:

- PAYMENT_CREATE
- PAYMENT_CONFIRM
- PAYMENT_REJECT
- PAYMENT_REVERSE
- CHEQUE_UPDATE
- CREDIT_AGREEMENT_CREATE
- CREDIT_AGREEMENT_APPROVE
- CREDIT_ALLOCATE
- CREDIT_VIEW

Role names must not be hard-coded into Core business logic.

Payment confirmation and credit agreement approval must enforce maker-checker separation.

## 9. Audit and Tenant Isolation

All payment and credit records must be organization-scoped.

Organization identity comes from the authenticated context, not from untrusted request bodies.

All financial state changes must be auditable.

No registered payment, confirmation event or credit transaction may be physically deleted.

## 10. Out of Scope

The following are excluded from MVP:

- Actual customer refunds.
- Full accounting and double-entry ledger.
- Official accounting voucher generation.
- Direct bank integrations.
- Treasury and cash management.
- Cheque book management.
- Complex guarantees and collateral workflows.
- Customer-level unallocated payments.
- One payment distributed across multiple Pre-Invoices.
- Physical release authorization.
- Loading, weighing and final invoice workflows.

No tests are authorized or executed by this ADR.

## 13. Consequences

Positive:
- Payments and credit remain separate domain capabilities.
- Partial payments and mixed financing are supported.
- Cheque instruments are not incorrectly counted as collected funds.
- Credit exposure is traceable.
- Financial history remains auditable.

Trade-offs:
- Payment and credit state handling is more complex than a single paid/unpaid flag.
- Credit exposure requires transactionally consistent ledger calculations.
- The existing customer credit limit requires explicit migration reconciliation.

## 14. Implementation Sequence

1. S6-A: Payments data model, Core, API and tests.
2. S6-B: Financial confirmation and cheque lifecycle.
3. S6-C: Credit agreements, credit transactions, Core, API and tests.
4. Integration verification across all three sub-stages.

Stage 6 implementation must not begin until this ADR and the detailed Stage 6 contract are accepted.
